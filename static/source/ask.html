What happens when you ask
A plain question in, checked facts out.
You ask
Is <token> safe? Give me the red flags.

Paste a token address from its page on dydt.ai.

Your agent runs
dydt-token-check skill
$ dydt token <token>
$ dydt token-holder-stats <token>
$ dydt token-holders <token>
$ dydt pools --base_address <token>
$ dydt wallet-created-tokens <creator>
You get (answer format)
RedMint authority, freeze authority, creator record
CautionTop 10 share, snipers, transfer tax
UnknownAny field dydt has no data for
OKChecks that passed

Each finding names the data it came from. No scores, no buy or sell advice.
